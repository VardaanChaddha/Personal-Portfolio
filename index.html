FIG.01 — PROFILE

I'm Vardaan Chaddha, a 2nd year B.Tech CSE Core student at University of Petroleum and Energy Studies. I like the part of building software where a blank page turns into something someone can actually click through — so I spend most of my time on the front end, sweating details like spacing, load time, and whether a button feels right to press.

Outside of coursework I build small projects to learn by doing, read up on design systems, and I'm slowly getting comfortable with backend basics so I can ship a full idea end-to-end. This site itself is one of those projects — built, styled and documented from scratch as part of Frontend Development.
